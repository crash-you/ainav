Platform Introduction

Forbes AI is the artificial intelligence section of the globally renowned business media Forbes. As an authoritative media in business and finance, Forbes provides readers with insights into AI technology applications in business, investment opportunity analysis, and market trend predictions through its unique business perspective and in-depth analysis.

Forbes AI focuses on the business value and investment prospects of artificial intelligence, not only reporting the latest developments in AI technology but also focusing on how these technologies translate into business value, create investment opportunities, and drive corporate innovation. The platform brings together top business journalists, investment experts, and industry analysts to provide valuable AI business insights for entrepreneurs, investors, and business leaders.

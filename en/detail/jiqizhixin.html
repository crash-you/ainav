Platform Introduction

Jiqizhixin (机器之心, Machine Heart) is China's leading artificial intelligence media and industry service platform, founded in 2014, focusing on core AI technologies such as machine learning, deep learning, and their industrial applications. As an important bridge connecting AI academia and industry, Jiqizhixin is committed to providing Chinese AI practitioners with the most authoritative and timely technical information and in-depth analysis.

The platform brings together AI technical experts, research scholars, and industry elites from around the world, presenting the latest developments in AI technology through professional content creation and in-depth technical interpretation. Jiqizhixin is not only a platform for AI news release but also an important force in promoting the development of China's AI industry, making significant contributions to the popularization and application of AI technology.

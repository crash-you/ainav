Tool Introduction

Perplexity is a revolutionary AI-powered search and Q&A engine designed to redefine how people access and understand information. Unlike traditional search engines, Perplexity doesn't just provide a list of links, but directly delivers accurate, comprehensive answers with verifiable information sources.

The platform combines advanced large language models with real-time web search capabilities, understanding complex query intentions, providing contextually relevant answers, and supporting in-depth exploration through follow-up questions. Perplexity is particularly suitable for researchers, students, professionals, and anyone who needs quick access to accurate information.

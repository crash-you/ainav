Platform Introduction

arXiv is the world's largest open-access preprint server operated by Cornell University. Since its establishment in 1991, it has been an important platform for the academic community to share the latest research results. Its artificial intelligence section (cs.AI) brings together the latest AI papers from researchers worldwide, providing first-hand research materials for academia and industry.

As an important infrastructure for academic research, arXiv allows researchers to share their research results before formal publication, greatly accelerating the dissemination of scientific knowledge. The AI section receives a large number of new paper submissions daily, covering various AI subfields including machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc., making it the best window for understanding cutting-edge AI research dynamics.

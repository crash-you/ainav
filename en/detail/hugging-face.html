Platform Introduction

Hugging Face is the world's largest open source community platform for machine learning models and datasets, known as the "GitHub of machine learning." Since its founding in 2016, Hugging Face has become the go-to platform for AI developers, researchers, and enterprises to access, share, and deploy machine learning models. The platform hosts over 1 million pre-trained models, 100,000 datasets, and 100,000 demo applications.

Hugging Face not only provides rich open source tools and libraries (such as Transformers, Datasets, Tokenizers, etc.), but also establishes an active community ecosystem that allows AI developers worldwide to easily collaborate, share, and improve machine learning models. From beginners to professional researchers, everyone can find the resources and tools they need here, promoting the democratization of AI technology.

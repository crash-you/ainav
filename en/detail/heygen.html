Q10: Are there ethical considerations with AI avatars?

A: Important ethical dimensions: Disclosure: Should you reveal avatar is AI? Varies by context. Corporate training/education: Generally acceptable without disclosure if content is accurate. Best practices: Be transparent when authenticity matters, focus on content value not deception, use AI as tool for efficiency. Reality: AI avatar videos are becoming normalized. Ethical use is about honesty and value, not the technology itself.

About This Tool

AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) is the world's leading AI music composition platform, specifically designed to help musicians, composers, and content creators generate original musical works. Through deep learning technology, AIVA has learned from thousands of classical music masterpieces and can create professional-grade music compositions covering various styles including classical, modern, electronic, jazz, and more.

As the first AI composer recognized by the music copyright society SACEM, AIVA not only creates complete musical works independently but also customizes music styles, emotional expression, and instrument configurations according to users' specific needs. Whether you're a professional music producer or music enthusiast, AIVA provides high-quality music creation solutions.

Key Features

  • Multiple music style creation (classical, modern, electronic, etc.)
  • Emotion-driven music generation
  • Custom instrument configuration
  • MIDI file export
  • Audio file generation
  • Real-time music editing and adjustment
  • Copyright protection and commercial use licensing
  • Collaborative composition features
  • Music template library
  • High-quality audio rendering
  • Batch music generation
  • API interface integration

About This Tool

Kling AI is an AI video generation tool launched by Kuaishou. Its 'Image to Video' function received significant upgrades in 2025, supporting more complex animation effects and adding AI sound effects to enhance video immersion. The tool can transform static advertising images into dynamic display videos, simulating smooth effects of product rotation or environmental changes.

As Kuaishou's important product in the AI video generation field, Kling AI fully leverages Kuaishou's rich experience and technical expertise in the short video domain. Whether for e-commerce product displays, social media content creation, or advertising marketing, Kling AI provides professional-grade video generation solutions that make static images "come alive".

Key Features

  • Image to Video conversion
  • Complex animation effects generation
  • AI sound effects auto-addition
  • Product rotation animation simulation
  • Environmental transformation effects
  • Smooth dynamic transitions
  • High-quality video output
  • Multiple animation style choices
  • Batch processing capabilities
  • Social media optimized output

Platform Introduction

WIRED AI is the artificial intelligence section of the globally renowned technology magazine WIRED. As a pioneer in technology media, WIRED has been focusing on the profound impact of technology on society, culture, and economy since its founding in 1993. Its AI section specifically covers the development of artificial intelligence technology and its transformative impact on human lifestyles.

WIRED AI is known for its unique humanistic perspective and in-depth reporting, focusing not only on technology itself but also on how AI reshapes our work, life, thinking patterns, and social structures. The platform brings together world-class technology journalists and commentators, providing AI coverage that has both technical depth and humanistic care, serving as an important window for understanding social changes in the AI era.

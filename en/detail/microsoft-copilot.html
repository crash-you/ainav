Tool Introduction

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant, deeply integrated into the Office suite, Windows system, and various Microsoft services. Based on advanced large language models, Copilot can understand natural language instructions and assist users in completing various tasks, from document writing and data analysis to code programming, comprehensively improving work efficiency.

As the core product of Microsoft's AI strategy, Copilot is not just an independent chat assistant, but an intelligent collaboration partner that can seamlessly integrate into users' daily workflows, providing contextually relevant help and suggestions. Whether creating presentations, analyzing Excel data, or writing code, Copilot provides professional AI support.

Tool Introduction

CapCut is a powerful AI video editing tool developed by ByteDance, designed specifically for short video creators and content producers. The platform combines advanced artificial intelligence technology with an intuitive user interface, providing users with comprehensive video production solutions from basic editing to advanced effects.

As a globally leading video editing application, CapCut not only offers traditional video editing features but also integrates multiple AI technologies, including smart editing, automatic subtitle generation, background removal, music matching, and more. Whether for individual creators or professional teams, CapCut enables easy creation of high-quality video content.

Smart Editing

AI automatically identifies video highlights and intelligently edits short videos, saving significant editing time.

Auto Subtitles

AI speech recognition technology automatically generates accurate subtitles, supporting multiple languages and styles.

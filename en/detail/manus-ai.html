About This Tool

Manus is a universal AI agent product released by Monica team on March 6, 2025. It can autonomously complete complex tasks such as automatic searching, multi-step actions, CAPTCHA solving, complex file processing, and generating structured outputs like tables and presentations. Manus represents the latest breakthrough in AI agent technology.

As a next-generation AI agent, Manus not only understands user intentions but also proactively plans and executes complex workflows. It breaks through the limitations of traditional AI tools with single functions, truly achieving the intelligent experience of "one command, complete the entire process." Whether it's data processing, document generation, or complex network operations, Manus can handle it all.

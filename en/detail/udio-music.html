Overview

Udio is an AI music generation platform launched in 2024, founded by former Google DeepMind researchers. It can generate high-quality complete songs with vocals, lyrics, and instrumentation from text prompts, rivaling professional production quality. Supports multiple music styles.

Key Features

  • Full song generation
  • Professional audio quality
